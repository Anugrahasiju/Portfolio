ESP32-Based Quantitative Physiotherapy Progress Tracker

Real-time joint range-of-motion (ROM) telemetry system measuring angular velocity, exercise repetitions, and kinematic deviation for clinical rehabilitation.

System Architecture & Capabilities

  • 6-DOF Kinematic Sensor Fusion: Integrates calibrated MPU6050 IMU with digital low-pass filtering (DLPF) and complementary pitch/roll algorithms to calculate joint angles with ±0.5° accuracy.
  • Low-Latency Wireless Telemetry: Transmits continuous joint trajectory stream over ESP32 BLE and WebSockets to clinician dashboards at 100 Hz update frequency.
  • Automated Score & Smoothness Engine: Computes rehabilitation compliance rate, angular velocity symmetry, and jerk-smoothness indexes for recovery benchmarking.
  • Interactive Visual Biofeedback: Real-time patient interface with dynamic progress gauges, target boundary warnings, and session replay charts.
